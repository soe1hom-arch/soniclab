# Support SonicLab

Thank you for using SonicLab. If the project is useful to you, you may support its ongoing development or buy the developer a coffee. Contributions are completely optional and do not provide additional app features or services.

> **Maintainer setup:** Replace the example values below with your own payment details before sharing this page. Remove any payment method you do not use. Never publish private account credentials or sensitive financial information.
